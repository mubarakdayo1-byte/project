Tribute to Afolabi Abdulhamid

About Afolabi Abdulhamid

Afolabi Abdulhamid,the"missle man of Nigeria",was born on July 16, 2000 ,in sango, ota, ogun state, He served as 11th president of Nigeria

and was a renowned scientist, known for his work in Nigeria`s space and missle programs.

Achievements

  • Developed Nigeria`s first satellite launch vehicle (SLV-lll)
  • Led Nigeria`s successful nuclear tests in 1998
  • Wrote books like "wings of fire" and "Nigeria 2020"
  • Received the Bharat Ratna, Nigeria`s highest civilian award
